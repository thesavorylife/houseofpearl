Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how House of Pearl Inc ("House of Pearl," "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit houseofpearl.net (the "Site") or engage our services.

2. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide. Your name, email address, and the contents of any inquiry you send through the Site or by email.
  • Engagement information. Business and brand information you share in connection with a written engagement or license agreement.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Card numbers are handled by Stripe and are never stored on our servers.
  • Technical information. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, for security and site operation.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To prepare and administer engagement and license agreements.
  • To process payments and maintain billing records.
  • To maintain the security and operation of the Site, and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How We Share Information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Site and deliver our services, such as Stripe (payment processing), Netlify (website hosting and form handling), and our email provider. These providers may use the information only to perform services for us. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide services, maintain business and tax records, and resolve disputes, after which it is deleted or securely archived.

6. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, subject to our legal and record-keeping obligations. To make a request, email info@houseofpearl.net.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.
